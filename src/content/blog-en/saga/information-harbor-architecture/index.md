Historically, the value of information was determined by its verifiability. In the era of early maritime navigation, survival favored those who relied on rigorous ship logs and verified signals, rather than coastal rumors. In the modern digital landscape, this principle remains unchanged: only structured data holds true significance. 

FÉLAG acts as an information harbor. The project filters out speculative noise, keeping the focus strictly on architectural logic, backtesting results, and risk management frameworks.

## Architectural Evolution: From Hypothesis to Protocol

The previous stage of the project's development was a period of stress-testing data transmission channels. Much like past cartographers weeded out unreliable routes, multiple distribution hypotheses were tested and evaluated within the project. Unviable solutions were discarded. 

The result of this cycle was a transition from chaotic testing to a deterministic content routing protocol, where each channel has a clearly defined function.

## Initial Distribution Matrix

Based on metrics analysis, a symmetrical matrix has been established, eliminating the cross-mixing of audiences and semantic layers.

**Russian-language track (RU):**
* **FÉLAG | Haven (VK):** A space for deep reading, project methodology, and community building.
* **FÉLAG | Profi (Setka.ru):** A professional platform for technical breakdowns and architectural case studies.
* **FÉLAG | Logbook (Telegram):** An operational journal featuring brief insights, logs, and metrics.

**English-language track (EN):**
* **FÉLAG | Haven (Hashnode):** A community for a systematic approach to markets and long-term SEO presence.
* **FÉLAG | Profi (Dev.to):** A platform for publishing architectural solutions and code snippets.
* **FÉLAG | Logbook (Telegram):** An operational journal for the international audience.

The primary hub for storing structured knowledge remains unchanged: [felag.online](/en/).

## FAQ

**1. What is the primary purpose of this information resource?**  
The exclusive documentation of systemic approaches, backtests, and architectural solutions in the field of algorithmic trading. The materials are strictly educational in nature.

**2. What is the content distribution matrix?**  
It is a routing system that separates content by language (RU/EN) and the functional role of the channel (Haven for community, Profi for technical case studies, Logbook for operational logs). This separation prevents the mixing of formats.

**3. Are trading signals or market forecasts published here?**  
No. Publishing forecasts, trading signals, or individual investment recommendations contradicts the project's architecture. Any mentions of assets are strictly analytical and historical in nature.

**4. How can I report a text error or a broken link?**  
Via the contacts listed on the [About the Project](/en/about/) page.

**5. Will an analysis of this distribution matrix's effectiveness be published?**  
Yes. Given sustained interest in this approach, a separate material will be prepared detailing the analytics and efficiency metrics of the chosen content distribution strategy.
