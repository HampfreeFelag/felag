## Introduction

The Spanish government is considering a bill granting the financial regulator CNMV powers to freeze cryptocurrency accounts without a court order. This is one of the harshest national approaches to crypto regulation in Europe. We examine the bill text and its consequences.

## What the bill proposes

1. **CNMV gets freeze powers.** The regulator can block crypto company accounts upon suspicion of money laundering or terrorist financing.
2. **Without a court order.** Decisions are made administratively, with appeal within 48 hours.
3. **Applies to all platforms.** Not only Spanish platforms, but also foreign ones providing services to Spanish residents.

## Context: MiCA is already in force

The European MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets) regulation entered into force in 2024 and establishes uniform rules for all EU countries:
- Licensing of crypto platforms
- Reserve requirements for stablecoins
- Mandatory disclosure of information

The Spanish bill goes beyond MiCA, adding administrative sanctions without judicial oversight.

Spain is the first EU country adopting a "freeze without court" approach for crypto assets.

## What this means for traders

1. **User risks.** Accounts can be frozen by administrative decision — without the ability to contest promptly.
2. **Compliance pressure.** Platforms operating in Spain may restrict access for residents to reduce regulatory risk.
3. **EU precedent.** If the Spanish approach is approved, other EU countries may follow.

## What to watch next

- Spanish parliament vote — timeline and chances of passage
- CNMV reaction — public statements from the regulator
- EU position — can the Commission challenge a national approach exceeding MiCA?
- Market reaction — crypto asset outflow from Spain or compliance strengthening

## Summary

The Spanish bill is one of the harshest national approaches to crypto regulation in Europe. Account freeze without a court order expands CNMV administrative powers, but creates risks for users. The precedent may influence the regulatory trajectory of the entire EU.

## FAQ

**Is account freeze the same as confiscation?**
No. Freeze is a temporary measure pending investigation. Confiscation requires a court order.

**How to appeal a freeze?**
The bill provides for appeal within 48 hours through an administrative court.

**Will this affect decentralized platforms?**
Likely not. DEXs have no jurisdiction and no centralized accounts to freeze.
